## Description

```text
Sync, export, and control your health data.

Unlock the possibilities in Apple Health. PulsHealth brings your health data into the files, databases, and AI tools you choose. Build a personal archive, create your own dashboards, or explore your habits in a whole new way.

Your database. Your AI tools. Your choice.

KEEP YOUR DATABASE IN SYNC

Keep new data flowing with automatic background sync. Connect your own PulsHealth server, use a compatible receiver, or sign in with an approved PulsHealth account. Save multiple database connections, each with its own sync progress.

EXPORT WHAT MATTERS

Choose your measurements, dates, and format. Export CSV for spreadsheets or JSON and JSONL for your own tools and projects. Save your files or share them wherever you choose. No account or database required.

YOUR HEALTH, IN EVERY DETAIL

Explore sleep, heart rate, activity, workouts, nutrition, and more. Choose individual readings or build custom aggregations, from hourly averages to daily totals.

BRING YOUR HEALTH DATA TO AI

Connect compatible AI assistants through your database and ask questions about your data. Explore how your sleep has changed, compare workouts, or look at activity trends over time.

FREE AND PRO

PulsHealth Free includes full Explore, unlimited manual exports of all types in CSV, JSON and JSONL from the most recent 30 days, and automatic sync of up to five health data types from the most recent 30 days. Raw readings and aggregates of the same type count once. Data Requests follow the same export history limit.

PulsHealth Pro unlocks unlimited sync types and all available history, subject to Apple Health permissions. Choose an optional auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription. Hosting is separate. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings.

Existing users who originally downloaded a pre-Pro version receive 30 complimentary days of Pro when eligibility can be verified after updating. No subscription is required and the gift does not renew or charge. Existing paid subscriptions keep their own billing terms.

BUILT FOR CONTROL

Choose what to export, what to sync, and where it goes. PulsHealth reads Apple Health without changing your records. No ads. No tracking. Open source.

Exploring and exporting work without an account. Database sync requires a compatible server or an approved PulsHealth account. New hosted accounts are subject to approval and availability. AI connections are configured through your database.

Learn more: https://pulshealth.com
Privacy: https://pulshealth.com/privacy/
Terms: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
```

## Review notes

Use the credentials in the separate sign-in fields. Recheck the actual
account, deletion flow and hosted connection before submission. The optional
throwaway receiver is omitted from this walkthrough.

```text
PULSHEALTH 2.2

PulsHealth reads Apple Health, explores it on-device, exports files, and syncs to a compatible server. It never writes Health data. Use the approved demo account in the sign-in fields for PulsHealth hosting; new account requests wait for approval. The account contains no real person's data.

FIRST RUN AND CONNECTION

1. Complete the four-page introduction. On the Health page, tap Continue and grant read access. On iOS 27, choose any available history permission. Finish with Start Exploring.
2. Open Sync and choose PulsHealth Managed Server (or Sync > Database to add a connection). Sign in at app.pulshealth.com using the approved account, then tap Connect this iPhone.
3. The browser returns to PulsHealth and the app tests the connection. Tap Save & Apply. No Health upload starts before applying the connection and selection.
4. On an empty device, add a Weight entry in Apple Health, enable Weight for sync and apply. Pull down on Sync and verify an uploaded sample.

FREE AND PRO

Explore is free. Manual CSV/JSON/JSONL exports include all types for the most recent 30 days; free automatic sync allows five distinct types for that period. Raw and aggregate of one type count once. Data Requests use the export history limit. Pro removes type/history limits, subject to Health permissions; hosting is separate.

The plan row in Settings opens the plan page. During a gift, tap Choose a Subscription to test purchases; Back to Your Gift keeps the existing access. Both durations provide the same access, with localized prices, renewal terms, Restore Purchases, subscription management, privacy and Apple's EULA. Older dates and a sixth sync type show upgrade prompts. Pending or unverified transactions do not grant paid access. Saved selections and uploaded data survive expiry.

EXISTING-USER GIFT

An Apple-verified original download build below 23 qualifies for 30 complimentary Pro days. The gift starts on a foreground opening that can verify and persist it; it requires no subscription and cannot charge or renew. Fixed dates and dismissal are stored in a device-only Keychain item. The plan page shows the end date. Paid subscriptions take priority and retain their billing terms. Sandbox reports original build 1.0 and qualifies, so the gift may appear during review. New production downloads receive Free.

EXPORTS AND REQUESTS

Export creates files locally; Share or Save to Files opens the share sheet. Settings > Create Request makes a link/QR without reading Health. Open or Scan Request lets the recipient review types, dates and destination before generating. Leave delivery off to test Generate & Share. HTTPS delivery is explicit; partial results require Send Available Data. There is no ongoing study enrollment or research consent process. Temporary exports are excluded from backup and removed after completed sharing and at launch.

ACCOUNT DELETION

Settings > Privacy & Data > Delete PulsHealth Account opens the account deletion page. Access stops immediately; data removal retries automatically and a private receipt confirms completion. Disconnecting a database only stops syncing.

NETWORK, PRIVACY AND BACKGROUND

NSAllowsLocalNetworking permits HTTP only for local-network receivers; public receivers require HTTPS. No arbitrary-load exception. Camera access only scans pairing/request QR codes; frames are not saved or sent and manual entry is available. puls://pair fills a draft after confirmation; Save & Apply commits it. puls://request requires review before reading or delivering. Health data is not used for advertising, marketing, data mining or sale. Background processing and HealthKit delivery keep enabled sync data current.

Source: https://github.com/PulsHealth/pulshealth
Privacy: https://pulshealth.com/privacy/
```

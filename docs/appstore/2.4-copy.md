# PulsHealth 2.4 release copy

Prepared for 2.4 (33). Submission status is recorded in the release record.

## What’s New

Explore, export, and sync together
Use the + menu on a health type to add raw readings, aggregates, or both to your export selection or Sync draft. Review your choices before exporting or applying sync changes.

Clearer browsing and exports
Browse separate health category cards with Workouts easier to find. Export starts with an empty selection, shows clearer progress, and resets after you share or save your files.

Ready for iPhone Duo
Explore your health data with the type catalog and selected analysis side by side on larger displays. Fold or unfold and keep your place, with layouts that adapt to the space available.

Better readability
Explore statistics and category labels adapt to larger accessibility text sizes.

## Reviewer walkthrough changes

Retain the existing approved demo account and other review instructions. Update
the heading to PULSHEALTH 2.4 (BUILD 33). The following checks supplement the
existing onboarding, subscription, account and privacy notes:

- Explore: expand a category and open a health type. Tap +, then Add to Export.
  Choose raw readings, aggregates (where available), or both. Add only changes
  the selection; Open Export shows it, and Create Export starts the operation.
- Export starts empty on a new launch. Add several types before creating a
  file. A completed Share or Save to Files removes the staged copy and returns
  to an empty selection. Cancelling sharing retains the completed export.
- Explore > + > Add to Sync uses a separate local choice sheet. Nothing uploads
  until Review and Apply on Sync. Individual pending changes can be undone.
- HealthKit deletion records are silently ignored in on-device exports; normal
  database sync continues to deliver deletion records.

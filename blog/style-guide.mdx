## Typography

### Headings

Use headings to structure your content hierarchically. Start with H2 for main sections (H1 is reserved for the title).

**Heading hierarchy:**
- H2 (`##`) - Main sections
- H3 (`###`) - Subsections
- H4 (`####`) - Sub-subsections
- H5 (`#####`) - Minor headings
- H6 (`######`) - Smallest heading

---

### Text Formatting

Regular paragraph text looks like this. Keep paragraphs focused and concise.

**Bold text** for emphasis on important terms.

*Italic text* for introducing new terms or light emphasis.

***Bold and italic*** for extra emphasis (use sparingly).

~~Strikethrough~~ for corrections or deprecated information.

`Inline code` for technical terms, variable names, or short code snippets.

## Interactive Components

### Accordion / FAQ

Collapsible sections for FAQs or expandable content:

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem title="What is VO2 Max?">
    VO2 max (maximal oxygen uptake) represents the maximum rate at which your body can consume oxygen during intense exercise. It's measured in milliliters of oxygen per kilogram of body weight per minute (mL/kg/min).
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="How accurate is Apple Watch?">
    Apple's validation studies report a correlation of approximately r = 0.89 with laboratory treadmill testing. Independent research has generally confirmed reasonable accuracy, with typical individual error margins of plus or minus 3-5 mL/kg/min.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="Can I improve my VO2 Max?" defaultOpen>
    Yes! Cardiorespiratory fitness is highly modifiable. With consistent aerobic training, most people can see 10-20% improvement in VO2 max within 3-6 months.
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

# Workout to Watch Support

Create, save, import, and schedule structured workouts on your iPhone for Apple Workout on your paired Apple Watch. Workout to Watch is a separate app, **Powered by Puls**; it does not require or automatically connect to a PulsHealth account.

## Contact

Email [support@pulshealth.com](mailto:support@pulshealth.com?subject=Workout%20to%20Watch%20support) with “Workout to Watch” in the subject. Include your iOS version and the action that failed. Do not send connection keys, payment details, or private health information.

## Free and Pro

Free includes:

- 10 saved personal workouts, with unlimited editing and reuse. Deleting one frees a slot.
- Two successful Smart Adds per calendar month. Failed generations and canceled previews do not use an allowance.
- Two upcoming scheduled sessions at a time. Past or completed sessions free their slots; two dates for the same workout count as two sessions.

Workout to Watch Pro is an auto-renewing monthly subscription, with a planned US launch price of **$1.99 per month**. The app shows Apple's current localized price and purchase terms before you subscribe. Pro removes those app limits and adds weekly repeat scheduling. You can continue viewing, editing, exporting, and deleting existing workouts after Pro expires.

Smart Add requires a supported device with Apple Intelligence and its on-device model available. Pro does not change device compatibility or Apple's Watch scheduling capacity. The optional assistant connector currently requires private enrollment and is not a promised subscription benefit.

## Restore or cancel

Open Settings in the app and find the **Workout to Watch Pro** section. Tap **Explore Pro** to open the purchase screen and restore purchases, or **Manage Pro subscription** to manage an active subscription. You can also [manage subscriptions with Apple](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions). Canceling stops the next renewal; Pro remains available through the paid subscription period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

## Watch scheduling

Allow scheduling when prompted, keep your paired devices connected, and open the iPhone app to sync. A saved workout and a successful submission to Apple are separate from confirming that it appears on your Watch. Sessions outside the current publication window stay locally until eligible. Apple's Workout app runs and records the session; Workout to Watch does not include a separate Watch app.

## Sharing and collections

Export a workout file to a recipient you choose, or create a public workout link that anyone with the link can open. A public link includes the workout's notes. Contact support if you need a public link removed.

For invited collaboration, use an iCloud collection. You choose the collection workouts to upload and manage members through Apple's sharing controls. This does not automatically upload your personal workout library. Copies saved by others can remain after you stop sharing.

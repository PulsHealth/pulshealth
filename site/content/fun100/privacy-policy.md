# Fun100 privacy policy

**Effective date: September 23, 2026**

This policy covers the Fun100 iPhone app and its iMessage app (together,
"Fun100"). Fun100 is a fitness challenge app for small groups of friends. It
describes what Fun100 stores, where it is stored, and who can see it.

## Who we are

Fun100 is developed by Sean Wade ("we", "us"). Fun100 is **not** part of
PulsHealth: it is a separate app by the same developer, and this policy is
published on the pulshealth.com website only for that reason. The PulsHealth
privacy policy does not apply to Fun100, and this one does not apply to
PulsHealth.

Questions about this policy or your data: [support@pulshealth.com](mailto:support@pulshealth.com).

## What Fun100 stores, and where

Fun100 has no servers of its own. Everything the app saves is stored in the
app's CloudKit public database, which Apple runs as part of iCloud. There is
no separate Fun100 server, and the developer does not operate any server for
Fun100.

In that database you are identified by an anonymous iCloud user record ID.
Fun100 never sees your Apple ID, your email address or your phone number.

Fun100 stores:

- **Your profile**: the display name and emoji you choose.
- **Challenges you create or join**: the challenge's name, dates, settings,
  house rules and its invite code.
- **Results you log**: weight, 1-mile run time, 20-minute ride distance and
  push-ups, each with its date and an optional note.
- **A fitness summary** built from the results you have logged, such as your
  best mile and your weight trend.

## Who can see it

- **People in your challenges.** Your profile, your logged results and your
  fitness summary are visible to the people in the challenges you join.
- **Anyone with a challenge's invite code.** A challenge's details are
  visible to anyone who has its invite code.
- **Other Fun100 users, technically.** Because this is a public database
  shared by everyone who uses the app, other Fun100 users could technically
  access these records, including people who are not in your challenges.
  Please don't enter anything in Fun100, including in names and notes, that
  you want kept private.
- **People in your Messages conversations.** Messages you send with the
  Fun100 iMessage app (for example an invite, a leaderboard or a result you
  just logged) are delivered by Apple's Messages to everyone in that
  conversation, like any other message, and include the challenge's invite
  code.

## Apple Health (optional)

Connecting Apple Health is optional. Fun100 works without it.

If you connect it, Fun100 reads your weight, workouts (runs and rides,
including distance over time), cardio fitness (VO₂ max), resting heart rate
and step counts. That data stays on your iPhone. Fun100 uses it to:

- show stats on your own Fitness page, and
- offer one-tap logging of a new weigh-in or a fast run or ride.

Nothing from Apple Health is uploaded unless you tap to log a specific result.
When you do, only that result (for example the weight or the mile time) is
saved, exactly like any other result you log, and it is visible as described
under [Who can see it](#who-can-see-it).

Fun100 never writes to Apple Health. Apple Health data is never used for
advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties or
data brokers.

## Notifications

Fun100's reminders and alerts are local notifications, scheduled on your
device.

## What we don't do

- No advertising.
- No analytics or tracking SDKs.
- No sharing of your data with third parties.
- No selling of your data.
- No tracking you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Deleting your data

- **In the app:** Settings → Delete My Data deletes your profile, your logged
  results and your fitness summary, and removes you from all challenges.
  Challenges you created remain for their other members; email
  [support@pulshealth.com](mailto:support@pulshealth.com) if you want one
  removed.
- **Apple Health:** you can disconnect Fun100 from Apple Health at any time
  in iOS Settings.
- **Anything else:** email [support@pulshealth.com](mailto:support@pulshealth.com).

## Children

Fun100 is not directed to children under 13.
